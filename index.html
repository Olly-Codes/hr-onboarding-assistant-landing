  1. Step 1

    Sign your offer letter

    Review the offer and sign it securely without leaving the chat.

  2. Step 2

    Sign your employment contract

    Sign the contract. Your manager countersigns, then we email you a link back to the chat.

    Read and sign company policies

    All policy documents come in one signing session.

  4. Step 4

    Share your feedback

    Finish with a short survey about your onboarding experience.

What to expect

  • One place for everything

    No forms to hunt down. The assistant tells you what’s next and gives you each document when it’s time.

  • Secure signing

    Documents are signed through DocuSign, so you get a signed copy you can keep.

  • Pick up where you left off

    Waiting on your manager? Close the page. We’ll email you a link when it’s your turn again.

Frequently asked questions

How do I start?

Select “Start onboarding chat”. The assistant asks you to verify yourself with your email or phone number, then takes you to your next step.

Which email or phone number should I use?

Use the ones HR has on file for you, usually the email that received your welcome message.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Verifying yourself in the chat is all you need.

Who do I contact if something goes wrong?

Email hr@hrautomationlabs.example and the HR team will help.
